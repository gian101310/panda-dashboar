PANDA TREND CONFLUENCE v1 — MT5

Package contents
  PandaTrendConfluenceV1.ex5       Read-only chart indicator
  PandaTrendConfluenceFeedV1.ex5   Licensed feed bridge (EA; no order code)

License
  Ask the Panda operator to approve your MT5 account and issue your individual
  Trend Confluence token. The token is not inside this ZIP. A copied package
  alone cannot read the Panda feed.

Install
  1. MT5 > File > Open Data Folder. Copy the indicator .ex5 to MQL5/Indicators
     and the feed .ex5 to MQL5/Experts. Restart MT5 or refresh Navigator.
  2. Tools > Options > Expert Advisors > allow WebRequest for
     https://pandaengine.app
  3. Attach PandaTrendConfluenceFeedV1 to one chart. Enable Algo Trading so
     MT5 permits the feed request. Enter the token supplied by the operator
     in LicenseToken. This EA has no trade functions or order execution.
  4. Attach PandaTrendConfluenceV1 indicator to a supported forex chart.
     The panel reads the licensed feed from the common MT5 data folder.

The panel uses confirmed H1 and M15 trend, ADX, local structure, and Panda
bias/Lines. Gap 9+ is the market path; gaps 5–8 are pullback review. A READY
or MARKET CONTEXT label is decision support, not an order instruction.
The MT5 panel focuses on the decision checks. The cTrader edition also draws
FVG, swing labels, pullback area, and a read-only risk plan on the chart.
