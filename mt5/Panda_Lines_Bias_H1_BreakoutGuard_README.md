# Panda Lines H1 Breakout Guard EA

This is a separate entry variant of `Panda_Lines_Bias_H1_ProfitTrail_EA.mq5`.
It retains the confirmed Panda Engine bias and H1 profit management, uses magic
`990145`, and leaves the original EA and its positions independent. The EA
does not draw its indicator calculations on the chart.

## Entry rules (defaults)

- The latest closed H1 candle must have crossed an opposing exported level and
  closed at least 30 pips beyond it. Buys break resistance; sells break support.
- Entry waits for a live pullback that still holds 5 to 20 pips beyond the
  broken level. A late price beyond that band is skipped.
- Entry must be no more than 50 pips from either Panda Lines plot.
- Initial SL is 5 pips beyond the broken level. The actual quoted entry-to-SL
  distance cannot exceed 30 pips. A slipped fill beyond that cap is closed.
- TP is placed at 1.5 times the initial risk. Another exported level inside
  that path, plus a 5-pip clearance, blocks entry. This also rejects nearby
  stacked resistance for buys or stacked support for sells.
- Spread must be at most 3 pips. The MT4 and MT5 prices must agree within 10
  pips. Both level export files must be updated within 20 minutes. Missing or
  stale data blocks entry.

The price levels come from `tbg_<PAIR>.txt` (PDH, PDL, PWH, PWL) and
`mt4_<pair>.txt` (exported box edges) in `Terminal\Common\Files`. The existing
`panda_score_<PAIR>.txt` is still required for confirmed bias. These three
files are not generated by the EA. Missing files cause no entry, including in
the Strategy Tester unless a compatible historical feed is supplied.

Pip size defaults to the standard forex digits rule. For gold, indices, or
other instruments, set `InpPipSize` explicitly and review every pip-based
default before use. The source is compiled but has not been attached or
validated through demo trades.
