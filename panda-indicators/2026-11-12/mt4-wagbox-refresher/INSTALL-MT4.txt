Panda WagBox Staggered Refresher

Compile Panda_WagBox_Staggered_Refresher.mq4 in MT4 MetaEditor and attach the
resulting EA to one controller chart, such as XAUUSD. Remove the old
AllChartsTimeframeRefresher or AllChartsTimeframeRefresher_v2 EA first.
If upgrading an attached staggered refresher, replace its EX4 and reattach
the EA on the controller chart so MT4 loads the new hourly schedule.
Keep Panda_Exporter_v2_BOS on USDCAD and WagBox on the pair charts.

Default behavior:
- Start one pass at :30 each hour on the Dubai PC clock, ahead of the hourly
  Telegram snapshot (normally sent after :01 of the following hour).
- If attached after :30, wait until the next hour's :30; do not start a catch-up
  pass immediately.
- On one chart at a time, switch D1 -> H4 -> H1 with 4 seconds between steps.
- Finish on H1, including charts previously left on H4 by the old refresher.
  If a switch fails or the EA is removed mid-sequence, restore that chart's
  starting timeframe as a safety measure.
- Wait 15 seconds before starting the next chart.
- Never overlap passes. If a pass is still running at the next :30, finish it
  first and wait for the following hour.

The EA does not place trades. It does not change chart templates or the Panda
exporter. Check the MT4 Experts log for "cycle complete" and confirm the
WagBox objects and exporter files update after the first pass.
