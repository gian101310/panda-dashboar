PANDA BOX AND LINES - MT4 (v1.34)

Copy Panda Box and Lines.ex4 into MT4's Data Folder > MQL4 > Indicators.
Refresh Navigator and attach it to a supported currency-pair chart.
For a chart with only this panel and its boxes, open a separate chart and apply
Panda Box and Lines Clean.tpl from MT4's Templates menu. Copy that template
into the terminal's Templates folder first. Keep existing exporter charts as
they are; their Scoring with Adv v1 and WagBox indicators draw extra panels
and boxes and may supply separate export data.
If replacing an earlier version, reattach the indicator or reload the chart
template once. The new version removes panel, box, and level objects saved by
older versions so only one panel remains after refresh.

The panel shows current and ADV D1/H4/H1 net scores, current gap/bias,
and a TMRW, WK, or MTH projected ADV gap/bias chosen by Dubai session.
ADV is a forecast; confirm it against the next live score.
The chart draws the three scoring boxes and Panda Levels (PD/PW/PM/PY high/low).
Use ShowBoxes, ShowPandaLevels, and ShowLevelLabels to control the drawings.
Drag the narrow strip at the top of the score panel to move it.

The indicator calculates from MT4 broker data across all 21 Panda pairs.
It needs H1 history for those pairs and does not read MT4 export files.
Panda Levels are price references only; they do not affect the score or bias.
The short scoring box follows Panda Levels V2 trading-day rollover and skips
Saturday and Sunday. Weekly and monthly windows retain their existing rules.
