PANDA STANDALONE RAW SCORE - MT4 (v1.30)

Copy PandaStandaloneScoringMT4.ex4 into MT4's Data Folder > MQL4 > Indicators.
Refresh Navigator and attach it to a supported currency-pair chart.

The panel shows current and ADV D1/H4/H1 net scores, gap, and bias.
The chart draws the three scoring boxes and Panda Levels (PD/PW/PM/PY high/low).
Use ShowBoxes, ShowPandaLevels, and ShowLevelLabels to control the drawings.
Drag the narrow strip at the top of the score panel to move it.

The indicator calculates from MT4 broker data across all 21 Panda pairs.
It needs H1 history for those pairs and does not read MT4 export files.
Panda Levels are price references only; they do not affect the score or bias.
Compare live values with Scoring with Adv v1 MT4 on the same broker.
