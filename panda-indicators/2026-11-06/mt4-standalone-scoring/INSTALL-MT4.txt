PANDA RAW SCORE — MT4 (v1.10)

Copy PandaStandaloneScoringMT4.ex4 to MT4's Data Folder > MQL4 > Indicators.
Refresh Navigator and attach it to a forex chart.

The panel shows only:
- BASE and QUOTE raw D1 / H4 / H1 scores
- ADV scores for both currencies
- BIAS

Hold the left mouse button on the title row marked [drag] and drag the whole panel. Its position stays in place during score refreshes. PanelX and PanelY set its initial location after reattaching/changing settings.

No EA, engine connection, token, source indicator, ATR, spread, gap, execution/confidence display, or chart boxes.

Scoring uses all 21 pairs and the existing box rules. Main boxes span two months/weeks/trading days with a one-period offset; ADV uses the same spans without that offset. H1 prices build all ranges. Above a box votes +1 for base/-1 for quote, below reverses, and inside votes zero. Positive/negative components stay separate, e.g. +3/-1. Bias uses the existing Panda gap and invalidation rules internally.

MT4 may need H1 history for all 21 pairs; load at least 3+ months when a history message appears. Symbols are added to Market Watch as needed. Prices are the broker's latest available quotes, like a regular indicator. Compare on the same broker and at the same time as the original.

Compiled with 0 errors and 0 warnings. Scoring matched 2,000 locked-engine test cases. Live mouse-drag/chart comparison remains unverified.
